# Privacy policy for Bubbles

Bubbles is a game for Even Realities G2 glasses. It collects no personal data.

## What the app stores

The game keeps a few small values so you can carry on where you left off: your
best score in each mode, the highest level you have reached, the mode you last
chose and whether you prefer the short aim guide. They are saved through the
Even app's local storage on your phone and never leave your phone. Removing
the app removes them.

## What the app does not do

- It makes no network requests. The game is bundled in the package and runs
  entirely between your phone and your glasses.
- It has no analytics, no advertising, no accounts and no tracking of any kind.
- It asks for no permissions. It does not use the glasses' sensors, microphone
  or camera, and it does not read your location, contacts or anything else on
  your phone.
- It never shares anything with the developer or with anyone else, because it
  has nothing to share.

## Changes

If a future version ever stores or sends anything more, this document will be
updated first and the change will be described in that version's notes.
